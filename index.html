Wiser Support

Wiser is a game you play against your own judgment: lock a prediction, live with it, and see how well your confidence matched reality.

Need help? We usually reply within 2 business days.

Contact support

Where are my bets stored?

On your iPhone only. Wiser has no account, and no bet content is sent to us, to analytics or to the subscription service.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: You → Settings → Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes everything. To delete your subscription records too, email us the Subscription ID shown under Settings → Privacy.

How do I cancel or manage Premium?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wiser. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period.

I bought Premium but the app says Free

Open You → Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this gambling?

No. A “bet” in Wiser is a falsifiable personal prediction. There is no money staked, no wagering and nothing to win.
